About The Photographer

Al Linsangan | Principal Photographer

With over a decade and a half behind the lens, Al Linsangan strips away color distractions to reveal raw depth, biodiversity, and geological geometry. Every frame captured under AL3 Photography emphasizes contrast, shadow play, and authentic narrative.

"When you remove color, you don't lose information—you gain focus. You see the soul of the subject, the quality of light, and the timelessness of the moment."
